# References and moves

Read whole at step 3; references are observed evidence for a decision, not permission to change PMO's business job.

## Two shelves

| Shelf | Where | What it answers | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SCREEN** | `docs/private/references/<screen-kind>/` | Which information, hierarchy and actions a real analogous screen puts together | PMO's required features, financial definitions, permissions or a reference's unseen states |
| **CRAFT** | Generated stageshow product studies and gold examples | How rows, controls, columns, menus and popovers are constructed; see `craft.md` | What a dashboard, approval queue or invoice screen should show |

- Use the screen kinds `dashboard`, `list`, `record`, `approval`, `form` and `phone`, because a shelf must be findable by the next Director.
- Before composing a kind with fewer than three verified references, collect three first from owner-supplied images or public product pages, docs and demos opened in a browser, because memory is not a reference shelf.
- Count only images actually present and opened at a readable size; pending copies and descriptions do not count, because a filename is not visual evidence.
- Keep one `index.md` per kind with `file | product kind | decision shown | leave behind | source/date | product identity | viewed`, because a later session needs both provenance and the limit of the take.
- Keep source identities and URLs private; use generic product kinds in tracked text, because this repository publishes everything tracked.
- Grow the shelf whenever a run finds a useful new decision, keeping unused images indexed separately, because future runs should start with evidence rather than rediscovery.
- A crop proves only what its bounds show; record unseen context and states as unknown, because a missing edge cannot prove a missing feature.
- The owner's finance-home images illustrate a figure band, one finding and a wide actual/projected/target chart; use their arrangement as evidence, because a picture cannot supply PMO forecast data.
- Open a CRAFT preview and inspect its DOM/computed styles for the needed part, because a hand-built recreation demonstrates construction rather than production business coverage.
- Treat a chat or editor study as part-level craft only, because its composition cannot justify the contents of an ERP screen.

## Pick one direction whole

- Group SCREEN references by structural decision, such as a work queue beside a summary versus a sequence of full-width sections, because “clean” and “modern” are not directions.
- Choose the direction that answers the job and owner's words across the whole target, because one liked card from each side produces an accidental composition.
- Record the direction, its anchors and one reason the other direction lost, because a successor needs a decision rather than a pile of inspiration.
- Take the chosen hierarchy, controls and scale as one structure rebuilt through PMO's components, because copying appearance imports a foreign identity.
- Preserve the frozen floor while fitting PMO-only content around that structure, because a reference with fewer capabilities cannot authorize their removal.
- Explore only when the direction remains undecided: real isolated branches, same data and floor, one coherent direction per branch, because drawn mockups do not prove the app.

## Evidence grades

| Exact decision visible in images actually looked at | Grade written with the claim |
|---|---|
| Two or more independent products | **Precedent** |
| One product, however many screenshots | **Example** |
| No product shows it | **My opinion** |

- Grade the exact decision, not the presence of a similar element, because a bare total does not support placing a total above a decision.
- Keep product identity in the private index to deduplicate captures, because several images of one product are still one example.
- Separate SCREEN grades from CRAFT observations; recreations and multiple gold demos do not increase the number of independent products, because implementation samples are not market precedent.
- Keep a no-reference judgement explicitly labelled “my opinion” and bounded by the job, floor and owner words, because an unsupported claim must not become an invented best practice.
- Require an observed screen for each structural move; collect the missing evidence before composing, because judgement about wording or alignment cannot stand in for a whole-screen reference.

## At most five moves

Write `problem + broken job step | chosen direction | screenshot(s) + exact decision | evidence grade | adds / moves / removes | floor IDs | visible proof`.

- Give each problem one move and cap the table at five, because a short set of structural decisions is reviewable against a whole screen.
- Record where displaced content lands and its discoverable link, because moving is only preservation when a person can still find it.
- Carry an explicit owner quotation for every drop, because a cleaner reference does not override the feature floor.
- Defer newly discovered out-of-scope work by job step and source evidence, because silence loses the finding while expanding scope loses the brief.
- Recompose a screen whose fix list exceeds about five rather than stacking patch rounds, because the direction itself needs another decision.
- Re-walk the applied render through all six job checks, because an individually successful move can break another part of the job.
- In the owner report, claim only differences visible in the actual before/after at its displayed scale, because planned changes and predicted business outcomes are not observed results.

**Complete:** the private index has three verified references for the kind, one direction is chosen, and every move names its observed screen, grade, floor impact and visible proof.
