From tender to ledger, in the order the money moves.

PMO Portal covers the project side of the business. Your ERP keeps the books.

Purchasing, with the budget in view before anyone approves.

A site engineer raises a request. Whoever approves it sees what it does to the project's budget for that category, and the PO, the receipt into a site warehouse and the vendor bill all stay attached to it.

  1. 1Budget (RAP) against spent and committed, for this category, before the request is approved. It updates as each PO and bill is approved.
  2. 2Within budget goes to the project's approver. Overhead or over budget goes to senior approvers.
  3. 3Nobody approves their own request. The server refuses it, and the approver can't also pay.

Billing against a signed Berita Acara, not against a promise.

Progress is recorded per BQ line. The claim becomes an invoice only once the client's signed completion certificate is attached to it, and down payment is recovered on each claim.

  1. 1Quantities claimed per BQ line: this claim, previous claims and to date.
  2. 2Down payment recovered on each claim, shown as its own line.
  3. 3No invoice until the signed Berita Acara is attached. Subcontractor bills can't exceed verified quantities either.

The directors' view: every project, in rupiah and dollars.

The dashboard reads from the same records the site and finance teams work in, so there is no weekly pack to assemble. Each contract keeps its own currency, side by side. An AI assistant answers questions from the same records.

  1. 1Contract on hand in IDR and USD, approvals waiting for you, projects at risk and overdue receivables.
  2. 2Budget used per project, split into spent and committed. Open a project for budget against actual by category.

What the server enforces, whatever the screen shows.

These rules live in the database and on the server, not only in which buttons are visible. A person who tries to go around the screen still gets refused.

  • Each person sees and does only what their role allows. A site engineer, a purchasing officer, a project manager and a finance officer open the same record and see different things.
  • Nobody approves their own request. The server refuses it, and the approver can't also be the person who pays.
  • Approval is routed by budget. Within budget goes to the project's approver. Overhead or over budget goes to senior approvers.
  • No invoice without a signed Berita Acara. A progress claim can't become an invoice until the signed certificate is attached. Down payment is recovered on every claim.
  • Subcontractor bills can't exceed verified quantities. The server stops a bill that goes above the quantities verified.
  • An append-only audit trail. Who did what, and when. Entries are added, never edited or deleted.

Sign-in is by email, magic link, or Microsoft 365.
