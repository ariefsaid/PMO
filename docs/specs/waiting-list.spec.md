# Spec: everyone is told what waits for them (fix-waiting)

**Status:** built from a first-time-person walk of the released build (v0.44.0), per role, on a phone, in Bahasa Indonesia. IDs `FR-WAIT-###`, `AC-WAIT-###`; jobs `JOB-WAIT-1..5`. Plan `docs/plans/2026-11-20-waiting-list.md`. Migrations 1560 (the waiting read), 1561 (the requester is told) and 1566 (the per-record assignment route the three operational record screens read).

## Job stories
1. When something needs my decision, I want to find it on my home, in my menu with a count, and in the approvals inbox, as the same list, so nothing waits unseen.
2. When I work in Finance, I want the Finance page and the home to show the same waiting list.
3. When I made a request, I want to see who it waits for and at which step.
4. When my request is returned or refused, I want to be told with the reason, in my language.
5. When I open a returned request again, I want what I typed and the reason it came back.

## Requirements (EARS)
- FR-WAIT-001 The system shall have ONE list of what a login can decide now. The home list, the Approvals badge, the Finance page and the Approvals inbox shall read it; its headline count shall equal its length; while any kind is unread or failed it shall show no count.
- FR-WAIT-002 The list shall cover every kind of decision a login can make: purchase approval, timesheet, expense claim, customer invoice, material-request step at each step, leave, site cash, daily report validation, work order issue, delivery record confirmation (a purchase received; a transfer received), client acceptance (BAST) confirmation, quantity change, subcontract certificate, equipment plan.
- FR-WAIT-003 A kind shall be ONE registered entry; a kind with no registry entry, no read or no inbox row shall fail a test.
- FR-WAIT-004 Where a kind had no per-person read, the database shall list it (`my_waiting_decisions`), with the waiting **assignment** for that kind: the people the record waits on. The assignment is deliberately narrower than the write gate where the two differ (an Ordered purchase is assigned to this project's own manager, though any non-requester Project Manager may receive it; a Sent transfer is assigned to its addressed receiver or a full destination keeper, though an Admin may receive any transfer), and it excludes light accounts wherever the deciding function refuses them. A named person is the person assigned to act next, not a promise that every actor the gate admits is listed. A material-request step counts whatever the organisation's notification setting says; the setting governs only whether a notification is also written.
- FR-WAIT-005 Anyone who can decide anything shall have the Approvals entry, with or without something waiting (`my_decision_standing`).
- FR-WAIT-006 A waiting record shall name who it is assigned to, in the reader's language: "Waiting for <name>, <role or capacity> (step n of m)" from the assignment the database computed. A single-decision record carries no step.
- FR-WAIT-007 When a material request, a daily report, a subcontract certificate or an equipment plan is returned or refused, the requester shall get a notification with the reason; approvals shall say what was approved. Titles the database writes shall be shown in the reader's language.
- FR-WAIT-008 A returned material request shall open with the reason it came back and the over-plan reason typed before.

## Decisions and open questions
- Client acceptance (BAST) confirmation is its own waiting kind with its own caller-filtered database read (`acceptances_awaiting_my_confirmation`, migration 1570), never the `delivery_record` kind (that is the purchase receipt) and never `my_waiting_decisions`. The read lists who MAY confirm (Draft on an Issued order, Admin/Finance, the author excluded unless the only active member); eligibility is not a promise the write succeeds — the document/line prerequisites and the separation-of-duties refusal stay in `confirm_work_order_acceptance`. The read carries no row cap and the client pages it, so a waiting count is never a truncated one.
- The material-request approver-inbox setting no longer hides a waiting step from the list, badge or inbox (it still governs notifications). This is a change from `material-request-approver-inbox.spec.md` (AC-MRI-013/014), made on the owner's brief: a step the database says is yours is yours.
- `work_order_issue` lists Draft orders that carry a value someone else set and that the viewer may issue; it does not list orders the viewer priced themselves. It is the waiting **assignment**: a Project Manager who does not manage the project, or the price author acting on their own won-value authority, can be admitted by `transition_work_order` without being assigned to the Draft. A zero-value Draft or a priced Draft with no recorded author is assigned to nobody.
- `transfer_receipt` is narrower than the receive gate on purpose (a senior role may receive any transfer; only transfers addressed to the viewer or kept by them are "waiting for" them). A light account is assigned only when it is the addressed receiver: a light keeper cannot read an unaddressed transfer (1480's restrictive SELECT), so it is not named.
- `delivery_record` is narrower than the receipt gate on purpose: any non-requester Project Manager may record the receipt, but the purchase is assigned only to its own project's manager. A light account cannot record a receipt (the transition refuses it), so no light person is assigned.
- A person is named while their profile is active — the account state these reads can see. The deciding functions additionally require full, current membership; an authentication-level restriction applied out of band is enforced by the write and is outside what the read observes, so the read is not an exact authorization-equivalence oracle.
- A customer invoice has one forward decision (Draft → Unpaid) and no return/refusal transition, so there is no requester outcome to tell for that kind; its requester who-next is the invoice's own single approval step, with the eligible Finance/Admin people named from the database (migration 1569).
- Not yet in the list (no per-person read or no waiting state): work-order candidates awaiting accept or reject, billing steps (already on the home, by role), and leave for someone who is not a login.
