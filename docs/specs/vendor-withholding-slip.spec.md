# Vendor withholding slips (bukti potong) — #911

**Status:** specification + implementation design for Director/owner sign-off; not implemented by this document.
**Depends on:** #876. **Consumer:** #898. **Timing:** required capability, **not a go-live item** (the #911 brief).
**Plan:** `docs/plans/2026-10-08-vendor-withholding-slip.md`.

## 0. Job story

When Finance has the bukti potong issued in DJP e-Bupot Unifikasi, I want to record its number and tax facts against all the vendor bills it covers, so that I can tell which withholding has a slip and reconcile the monthly tax register without creating the same slip twice or opening ERPNext.

## 2. Fixed Director decisions — do not re-open

- **DD-BUPOT-1:** PMO **records** the slip number issued by the tax system (DJP e-Bupot Unifikasi); it never generates numbers. Internal UUIDs are identities, not tax document numbers.
- **DD-BUPOT-2:** a slip is its own record: number, date, PPh type 23 / 4(2), base, amount, vendor; it links to **one or more** vendor bills of the same vendor and PPh type. Slip amount equals the sum of linked bills' withheld amounts. A bill's withholding is “slipped” when covered.
- **DD-BUPOT-3:** slips feed the tax register (#898). Its columns and grains are defined in §6 now. Slip data is **PMO-only**, never pushed to ERPNext.

### Implementation decisions within that scope (Director-ratifiable, not owner questions)

1. **Exact reconciliation:** zero tolerance, exact two-decimal numeric equality. One slip covers each linked bill's entire withholding; no partial allocations or multiple simultaneous slips per bill. The external document's amount is entered, not manufactured from the selection. Show the selection sum alongside it. A difference of 0.01 refuses the write. No rounding away a mismatch; correct the authoritative source or the slip entry.
2. **Base:** enter the PPh taxable base exactly as printed on the slip, positive, finite, at most two decimals; amount is positive and no greater than base. Do not infer it from VAT, the vendor default, or gross bill totals; tax-object bases can differ. No rate calculation/validation against a vendor default.
3. **Eligibility:** positive bill withholding only; same org, current procurement vendor, PPh type and currency, not ERP-cancelled and not in a Cancelled procurement. Paid and terminal non-cancelled cases are eligible: issuing evidence does not depend on vendor settlement or case writeability. Negative return bills remain visible as `return-review` in the register, not selectable; this slice does not invent negative DJP slips, statutory cancellation, or credit-note netting. Zero withholding is `not-required`.
4. **Unknown bill type:** where `withheld_pph_type` is null, Finance explicitly confirms that **each selected unknown-type bill** belongs to the slip's chosen PPh type, using the external slip as evidence. Store `type_source = declared` and the original null type in its link snapshot. Known types must match; no confirmation overrides a known conflict. Never write the declaration back to the ERP-owned bill or infer it from the vendor's current default. This accommodates E2 without blocking template-path bills or adding a GL classifier.
5. **Lifecycle:** saving records an already-issued slip, not a draft for tax issuance. Active slips allow correction of **number/date/tax period only**, with a required correction reason and optimistic revision check. Vendor/type/currency/base/amount/links are immutable. Correct those by **voiding and recording a replacement**. Voiding releases bill coverage atomically but retains the header and all original links/snapshots. Void in PMO means “do not use this PMO entry”; it does not claim DJP cancellation. Void is terminal, with actor/time/reason; no delete, restore, or statutory approval workflow.
6. **Roles / SoD:** active Finance/Admin may record, correct metadata, and void, including their own entries. Other active org members may read within the linked source-row visibility. No additional maker/checker rule: recording external evidence neither authorizes payment nor submits a return; the existing bill/payment controls are untouched. No Operator override outside an active Finance/Admin org role.
7. **Number:** preserve the trimmed issued text (1–100 characters, no control characters); do not impose an unverified DJP digit-mask. Uniqueness is per org on `lower(btrim(slip_number))` **among active entries**. A duplicate active number points the user to the existing record. After void, a new intent may record corrected facts under the same issued number; the old voided entry and its links/history remain visible. Otherwise a misentered base/amount could never be corrected without inventing a false number. Correcting a mistyped number frees the incorrect spelling without erasing history.
8. **Dates and period:** slip date is a calendar `date`, not an instant, and cannot exceed today in the organization's timezone. Store a separately entered **tax period** (first day of month) because issue date and return period need not agree. Prefill from the slip date month until edited; no future period relative to the org's current month. This is a recording convention, not an assertion about statutory filing deadlines.
9. **Currency:** one slip has one explicit ISO-4217 currency, derived from the selected bills and displayed on every money field. No mixed-currency links or conversion. Register totals group by currency; statutory IDR conversion/export is outside #911.
10. **Later bill changes:** snapshots preserve what was covered at recording time. Re-evaluate against current bills on read. Changed withholding, vendor, known type, currency or cancellation makes the active slip `needs-review` and **all** of its links cease to qualify as slipped until corrected/voided. An unknown type that later becomes the confirmed type remains valid; a different type does not. Do not block ERP refreshes or silently change an issued slip. Mismatched links remain reserved until void, so a second slip cannot conceal the discrepancy. Missing/unreadable source facts are `unavailable`, never “not recorded”.
11. **Bounds:** 1–100 distinct bills per write; cross-procurement/project grouping is allowed. Candidate lists and register lists use stable keyset paging, at most 100 rows per page; no silent top-1000 truncation, no per-bill fetching. A tax system document covering more than 100 bills is refused with an explicit limit message, never split automatically.

## 3. Scope

**In:** native and ERP-mirrored vendor bills; separate slip header and bill-link snapshots; ledger/bill capture, view, metadata correction, void; auditable coverage; tenant-safe register read seam.

**Out:** number generation; DJP API/issuance/filing/cancellation; ERPNext writes/outbox commands; bill/payment/GL changes; tax-rate assessment; attachments/OCR; return netting; statutory return export/conversion; building #898's register screen. This is not a go-live dependency.

## 4. Requirements (EARS)

- **FR-BUPOT-001 (ubiquitous)** — The system shall record a separate vendor withholding slip with the DD-BUPOT-1/2 fields, currency and tax period, accepting only the validation conventions in §2 and never minting its issued number.
- **FR-BUPOT-002 (event-driven)** — When an active Finance/Admin records a slip, the system shall atomically link 1–100 distinct eligible bills of its vendor/type/currency and store each bill's withheld amount, original type, procurement and currency as an immutable snapshot.
- **FR-BUPOT-003 (unwanted)** — If the slip amount differs from the exact sum of linked withheld amounts, or a bill fails §2 eligibility, then the system shall refuse the entire record, leaving no slip, links or audit success behind.
- **FR-BUPOT-004 (conditional)** — Where a selected bill's PPh type is unknown, when Finance/Admin explicitly confirms its type from the external slip, the system shall store that declaration on the link without changing the bill; without that confirmation it shall refuse.
- **FR-BUPOT-005 (ubiquitous)** — The system shall reserve at most one active slip link per bill, enforce org-scoped active-number uniqueness, and make an exact retry of a record request return its original slip without adding links or history; reuse of its intent UUID for different input shall be refused.
- **FR-BUPOT-006 (event-driven)** — When Finance/Admin corrects an active slip's metadata with its current revision and a nonblank reason, the system shall change only number/date/tax period and increment its revision; stale or voided edits shall be refused and unchanged edits shall create no new history.
- **FR-BUPOT-007 (event-driven)** — When Finance/Admin confirms void with a nonblank reason and current revision, the system shall retain the entry and snapshots, stamp the real actor/time, release all its links in the same transaction, and increment its revision; an identical retry shall not repeat the operation.
- **FR-BUPOT-008 (state-driven)** — While an active slip still reconciles to every current linked bill, the system shall mark those bills' withholding “Slipped”; while any link no longer reconciles it shall mark the slip and all its linked bills “Needs review”, preserve its reserved links and show the reason; while source facts cannot be read it shall show “Unavailable”.
- **FR-BUPOT-009 (ubiquitous)** — The system shall derive uncovered, not-required and return-review states independently of bill payment status, and shall never interpret a voided slip as coverage.
- **FR-BUPOT-010 (ubiquitous)** — The system shall enforce active membership, roles and org identity server-side, stamp org/actors from trusted context, enable/force RLS on both tables, permit source-visible own-org reads only, and provide no direct client INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE path.
- **FR-BUPOT-011 (event-driven)** — When a slip or link is recorded, corrected or voided, the system shall retain actor/time and record classified change history and an audit event sufficient to trace its selected bills and reasons, without hard-deleting earlier evidence.
- **FR-BUPOT-012 (state-driven)** — While viewing an invoice in the procurement Documents ledger, users shall see its bukti potong state and may open its complete slip and linked-bill list; Finance/Admin shall have the permitted capture/correction/void actions even on Paid cases, without removing e-Faktur actions or the existing withholding breakdown.
- **FR-BUPOT-013 (event-driven)** — When the record form opens from a bill, the system shall preselect that bill and show its vendor/currency, allow choosing other eligible bills across procurements through paged search, show unknown-type confirmations and exact selected totals, and retain the user's inputs on any refused/uncertain save.
- **FR-BUPOT-014 (ubiquitous)** — The system shall expose the two #898 register grains/columns in §6 under caller RLS with stable pagination, include void/review/uncovered records as explicit states and prevent duplicated slip totals across bill links.
- **FR-BUPOT-015 (ubiquitous)** — Slip writes shall stay PMO-local on both ownership modes, shall not route through the ERP adapter or create a pending-push state, and shall not change bill tax/net payable/cost/payment facts.
- **NFR-BUPOT-001** — Arithmetic uses exact DB numerics and integer minor units in the UI; writes reject excess precision/non-finite input before coercion. SQL sum overflow produces refusal, not truncation.
- **NFR-BUPOT-002** — Candidate/register reads are paged server-side with deterministic date/id ordering; the ledger fetches coverage once per page of invoice IDs, never once per row. Unknown/error/loading states cannot fabricate absence or zero.
- **NFR-BUPOT-003** — New copy ships in English/Bahasa Indonesia, with shared form/dialog/table primitives and DESIGN.md tokens; 360px/mobile and keyboard flows remain usable, dates never timezone-shift, and WCAG-AA/axe and visual gates cover the new states.

## 6. Contract handed to #898 — implement the read seam, not the register screen

**Do not merge these grains or sum header money once per linked invoice.** Store/output raw calendar dates and raw currency amounts. Report period means `tax_period` for slips; bills expose `invoice_month` only as an operational default, explicitly not an inferred statutory period for unissued withholding. No “reported to DJP” flag is invented.

### A. `vendor_withholding_slip_register` — one row per slip ID, including voided

| Column | Meaning/type |
|---|---|
| `slip_id`, `vendor_id` | UUID identity and supplier company identity |
| `slip_number`, `slip_date`, `tax_period` | issued text, date, recorded return month (date at month start) |
| `pph_type`, `currency` | `pph23`/`pph4_2`; ISO-4217 |
| `tax_base`, `withheld_amount` | exact recorded slip facts, numeric(14,2) |
| `status`, `validation_state` | `active`/`void`; `reconciled`/`needs-review`/`unavailable`/`void` |
| `invoice_count`, `visible_invoice_count` | recorded expected links; currently source-visible links (never accept a hidden partial aggregate) |
| `linked_withheld_at_record`, `linked_withheld_current`, `difference` | recorded snapshot sum; current sum/difference only when all sources are visible; difference = slip amount minus current sum |
| `has_declared_type` | whether Finance classified any originally unknown bill type |
| `created_by`, `created_at`, `revision`, `updated_at`, `voided_by`, `voided_at` | attribution and optimistic concurrency metadata; IDs are resolved by the consumer under normal profile visibility |

Header totals: active + reconciled rows only, group by tax period/PPh type/currency. Review/unavailable rows are a separate exceptions count, **never silently included as reconciled**; voided rows never contribute. #898 joins vendor display data under normal company RLS, not a copied public tax-identity field. No taxpayer identifier/name snapshot is added by #911.

### B. `vendor_withholding_bill_register` — one row per vendor invoice ID

Columns: `invoice_id`, `procurement_id`, `project_id`, `vendor_id`, `vi_number`, `reference_number`, `invoice_date`, `invoice_month`, `currency`, `withheld_pph_type` (nullable original), `withheld_amount` (signed current), `erp_docstatus`, `erp_cancelled_at`, `case_status`, `active_slip_id` (nullable), `slip_number`, `slip_date`, `tax_period`, `resolved_pph_type` (original or explicit linked declaration), `type_source` (`bill`/`declared`/null), `linked_withheld_at_record`, `coverage_state`, `review_reasons` (stable enum keys translated by UI).

Coverage states: `not-required`, `return-review`, `not-recorded`, `slipped`, `needs-review`, `unavailable`. A voided-only link does not set `active_slip_id`; its history remains on slip detail. An active mismatched link retains that ID and is not “slipped”. Readers can drill down via immutable link rows for any slip, including voids. Bill-register totals use the bill's own withholding once, grouped by currency and known type; untyped, return-review and unavailable are explicit exception buckets. This is **not** a payable/GL balance or proof of return submission.

Read APIs: invoker views under underlying RLS; typed repository keyset pages ordered by `(tax_period, slip_id)` for headers and `(invoice_date NULLS LAST, invoice_id)` for bills. Header period predicates are month dates; invoice date nulls require an explicit cursor phase. All lists cap pages at 100 and continue until the requested page/end, never treating one capped response as the entire register. §6 is the shared handoff to #898; its eventual export/layout is separately specified there.

## 7. UI placement and owner questions

Operate-mode design: preserve the incumbent procurement identity (E6). On each Invoice row, add the quiet bukti potong state **beneath the existing withholding breakdown in the Amount cell**, not another wide table column. Keep e-Faktur separate. Row menu: “Record bukti potong” for an eligible uncovered bill; “View bukti potong” for any active link; retained voids via a “Bukti potong history” action. Detail opens within the procurement page with a URL-backed `bupot=<uuid>` selection so reloads/deep links work. Capture is a shared EntityFormModal; the complete grouped slip is a secondary detail panel, not a new top-level navigation module.

Capture: vendor/currency summary → issued number/date/tax period/type → base and issued amount → paged bill selector with source amounts and per-unknown-bill confirmation → exact sum/difference → Record bukti potong. Reasons and failed writes stay in the originating correction/void dialog. View-only access remains useful. On void confirmation explain that it changes PMO coverage, **not** DJP issuance/cancellation.

**OPEN OWNER QUESTIONS: none.** Number ownership, grouping and register ownership are settled by DD-BUPOT-1..3. Precision, lifecycle, role enforcement, paging and unknown-type handling above are implementation decisions, not commercial questions. Spec sign-off and the directional owner sketch-glance remain approval checkpoints before build; this planning-only delivery is not a claim that those approvals or tests have occurred.
