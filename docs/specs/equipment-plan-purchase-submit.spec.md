# Submit an equipment-plan purchase

FR-EPS-001: When an active holder of the organisation's ordering right submits a Draft purchase generated by an Approved equipment plan in their organisation, the system shall submit the purchase through the existing approval route.
FR-EPS-002: The requester of a generated purchase shall be the plan author.
FR-EPS-003: While a purchase has been submitted by a person other than its requester, when that submitting person attempts to approve it, the system shall refuse the approval, including after a role change.
FR-EPS-004: The system shall retain all existing submission, approval, field-account, organisation and financial checks.

- AC-EPS-001 (unit): Given the server's provenance context, when the screen computes actions, then the Buyer receives Submit only for an eligible Draft and a submitting actor receives no approval action.
- AC-EPS-002 (pgTAP): Given a generated Draft and ordering right, when the Buyer submits, then it becomes Requested and its requester remains the plan author.
- AC-EPS-003 (pgTAP): Given an ordinary Draft, when another requester’s Buyer tries to submit it, then the action is refused.
- AC-EPS-004 (pgTAP): Given the Buyer submitted, when they try to approve, including after elevation to Admin, then approval is refused; an independent approver succeeds.
- AC-EPS-005 (pgTAP): Given another organisation, when accessing the purchase or provenance context, then no submit right or provenance is disclosed.
- AC-EPS-006 (e2e): Given the equipment officer’s approved plan generated two Draft purchases, when the Buyer opens Purchasing and submits each, then both await normal approval with the equipment officer retained as requester.
