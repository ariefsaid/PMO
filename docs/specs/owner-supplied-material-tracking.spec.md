# Owner-supplied material tracking

Draft for owner sign-off · #3010 · 2026-11-27. Supersedes the dispatch's initial identity assumption: **owner order number identifies a work order; a location sheet identifies a section**. No customer data belongs in this document or its tests.

## Job story and scope

When I keep the material record as Material Coordinator, I want to load the owner's workbook once, inspect each location's material stages, and compare work orders, so I can explain what was asked, set aside, received, issued and left over and complete reconciliation with the owner. The demonstration succeeds when one previewed command loads the accepted records and the app reproduces their quantities and workbook percentages; refused records remain visible in a private report and prevent a claim of complete parity.

**Phase 1 scope:** opening stage totals, loader and read/reconciliation screens; live stage and movement writers, the two-session import race harness and the AC-009 benchmark are phase 2.

## Observed foundation and decisions

Checked at dev commit `54e4cd6480`: `1162_client_requisitions.sql`, `1163_client_requisition_rpcs.sql`, `2040_material_coordinator_rights.sql`, `2229_native_client_pickup_receipts.sql`, `1173_work_order_section_materials.sql`, `1169_work_order_sections.sql`, `1382_work_order_areas_groups.sql`; DALs `clientRequisitions.ts`, `workOrderSections.ts`, `closeoutChecklist.ts`; `clientRequisitionRollup.ts`; `ClientMaterialSection.tsx`; CLI `scripts/pmo.mjs` and its runbook. These are source observations, not a claim about a running database.

Owner reservations already have an event/reference/date model in client requisitions. Reuse it; do not use `stock_reservations`, which means stock held internally. Add owner warehouse and section-plan linkage. Existing pickup posting attaches a work order to a zero-cost receipt; **the ledger joined to its posted document can answer received for a work order**. It cannot attribute that receipt to one of several section plans without an explicit line link. Add that link for every imported movement; do not infer section from warehouse. Live receipt writers must also accept section-plan attribution when used for this pipeline.

`work_order_sections` fits the location sheet: scoped child with code/name and material plan. `work_order_areas` is an organisation-level geographic catalogue, not the location child. Put owner cost code on the section. Keep planned quantities in `work_order_section_material_plan`; link requisition lines to that row rather than storing a second plan counter.

Pre-go-live history is imported as opening stage totals per section material plan, with an as-of date and bounded display-only source evidence. No stock documents, requisitions, events or ledger rows are posted by material import. After go-live, linked live records add to these opening totals through the shared stage read. Existing stock posting and requisition guards remain unchanged.

Use a separate material reconciliation scale and section status. The general closeout checklist is an ordered list of independent obligations with evidence and completion dates; treating its arbitrary count as a percentage would change meaning. A material reconciliation section can coexist with that checklist. Work-order reconciliation displays the lowest completed section percentage, plus section counts; location rows retain their exact source state.

## Requirements (EARS)

- **FR-001** When a workbook is previewed, the loader shall match work orders by trimmed owner order number within the named project, matching either `number` or `client_po_number` and refusing disagreement or multiple matches; the material import shall refuse a missing work order with material-work-order-missing and write nothing for its section. The loader shall create missing work orders beforehand through the existing work-order import, with commercial inputs explicitly supplied by the operator in the private mapping. Only Draft and Issued orders accept section imports; Closed and Cancelled orders are refused.
- **FR-002** When a location is loaded, the loader shall match its section by `lower(trim(name))` within that work order, preserve internal spaces, refuse ambiguous matches, create missing sections with deterministic collision-checked codes, and keep owner cost code on that section. It shall never collapse locations sharing an order number.
- **FR-003** When catalogue rows are loaded, the loader shall match items by trimmed code only, preserve leading zeros, trim unit whitespace, retain an existing name with a note and refuse a mismatched unit after trim and upper-case normalization instead of overwriting. It shall add organisation-defined categories (unique code, name) independently of the zero-cost stock tag. Category is recorded on the section plan because the same item can serve different work packages; an item may have an optional default category.
- **FR-004** When a plan is loaded, the loader shall upsert by section plus item, preview old/new quantity, and update quantity to the approved workbook value; matching unchanged rows shall be skipped. Repeated section/item rows follow duplicate_items: refuse by default, or sum under explicit mapping policy while retaining every source row in evidence. A line with planned 0 and no stage quantity is an empty_line: skipped and counted, not refused. Zero planned quantities with movements shall be refused for explicit correction rather than silently discarded.
- **FR-005** When owner-stage evidence is imported, the system shall store requested, set-aside and movement opening totals per planned-material line, with the source references, dates, owner warehouse codes and quantities retained verbatim as bounded display-only evidence. Each evidence value shall be scalar text of at most 4096 UTF-8 bytes and the complete object at most 32768 bytes. Import shall create no requisitions or requisition events; linked live requisitions add to openings after go-live.
- **FR-006** While displaying stages, the system shall add opening totals to posted ledger/document movements linked to section plans. Reversal or cancellation shall remove the reversed live contribution; internal warehouse transfers shall not count as another receipt. Closing = received + transferred-in − issued + returned − transferred-out − owner-return. Import shall reject negative closing unless the section explicitly chooses movements_win, in which case its result shall be flagged.
- **FR-007** When computing workbook progress, the system shall calculate per-line set-aside/planned and received/planned, issued/received and closing/received, cap only the displayed ratio above at 100%, and exclude undefined zero-denominator ratios from arithmetic means. A flagged negative closing shall retain its quantity but have an absent closing percentage, excluded from section and work-order means. Empty means shall display absent, with a source-compatible zero available in the validation report. Work-order percentages shall average eligible line percentages across its sections, not average section means or divide mixed-unit totals. Requested/planned uses the same denominator rule. Negative closing stock shall be refused on import unless movements_win is explicit (FR-006), not hidden by a percentage cap. Returned and transferred quantities remain visible alongside the four workbook percentages.
- **FR-008** When reconciliation is configured, an Admin shall define ordered uniquely named steps and monotonically increasing percentages ending at 100; defaults shall be Prepared 20, Checked 40, Submitted 60, Agreed 80, Closed 100. When the Material Coordinator records a section's step, the system shall audit actor/date and show the work-order conservative minimum. Names and percentages from an approved private mapping override defaults; moving backwards requires a reason. Scale configuration shall retain unchanged step identities and refuse to drop, rename, reorder or change the percentage of a step currently used by a section; archived prior steps remain available to historical events.
- **FR-009** When `node scripts/pmo.mjs material load /private/material.xlsx --project <uuid> --map /private/material-map.json` runs, it shall preview by default; only `--confirm` shall write. It shall use the ordinary configured CLI identity/organisation and report its target before preview, never use a service credential. The project and zero-cost tag must exist. Missing unit codes shall be previewed and created through catalogue entries using mapping definitions, defaulting to code as name and whole_only false; existing normalized unit codes shall be retained. Mapping shall not create locations or projects. Mapping requires zero_cost_tag_name and may define the reconciliation scale; a different scale is applied through om_material_set_scale before section confirmation. The existing work-order setup workflows must be configured before missing work orders are created.
- **FR-010** When the same import resumes or repeats, the server shall lock its stable source identity, compare canonical payload hashes and skip identical operations including calls by another authorised Admin. A changed hash shall update plan quantity and opening totals with before/after evidence. Each section shall commit atomically except that a unit mismatch refuses only its line and marks the section incomplete; compatible lines remain accepted. Catalogue entries report individual refusals. Projected catalogue entries and reconciliation scale shall be validated in the same rolled-back preview; confirmation shall require the projected scale to match current configuration. Every result shall carry reason_code and human reason fields; a refusal shall identify its rule and offending field/value. Preview shall make zero writes and confirmation shall revalidate.
- **FR-011** When parsing source cells, the loader shall report sheet, row, column, class and reason for non-numeric stage quantities (including Check and Excel errors), missing or conflicting formula caches, invalid numeric precision, unmapped sheets and inconsistent stock arithmetic. Compound dates and document numbers are retained verbatim as evidence; they are not split or guessed. The loader shall never execute formulas, macros or external links. Daily-report, closing-stock and summary sheets are excluded from posting and used only for supported title/scale extraction; they are not cross-checked for quantity parity. Calibration and ancillary checker sheets shall be explicitly excluded. Unmapped non-template sheets shall be reported.
- **FR-012** When existing records overlap, the preview shall match exact work-order references, sections, item codes and plans, refusing ambiguous identities. Import shall update only its source-owned opening totals and plans, shall never clear a tenant or delete ledger history, and shall report conflicting source ownership without writing the section.
- **FR-013** When opening a work order, its existing material surface shall show a section selector and paged stage table with item/unit/category, planned, requested, owner set-aside, received, issued, returned, moved and closing quantities and percentages. Owner cost code and reconciliation belong to the selected section. The table shall show Loaded position as of <date> and per-line source evidence. When skipped_source_rows is above zero it shall say how many source rows were not loaded and show the reasons in plain words. Existing reservation/receipt actions remain reachable.
- **FR-014** When opening Work Orders, a Materials view shall show one row per work order with eligible-line stage means, reconciliation, section count and drilldown. Expanding a work order shows each location's percentages and reconciliation step from om_material_section_rollup for comparison to the workbook. Both reads shall expose partial-load metadata: section skipped counts/reasons and work-order partial_section_count, replaced on each section reload. The count of stock movements absent from location figures shall include only items with a live same-organisation zero-cost tag. Quantities shall be totalled only within a unit. Neither screen shall present mixed units as physical totals.
- **FR-015** While a member reads these records, the server shall enforce active same-organisation membership and existing project visibility. Admin manages catalogue/configuration and bulk import. Material Coordinator and existing requisition recorder roles maintain owner stages and reconciliation; receipt/issue/return posting retains stock-keeper rights. Plan import is Admin-only, and existing manual plan rights remain. Destructive removal is Admin-only and referenced history cannot be deleted. Real-role `can()` gates mirror server checks; impersonation stays read-only.
- **FR-016** Where an organisation renames a stage, all headings/actions shall use #2529 terminology. Neutral defaults: Requested, Owner set-aside, Received, Issued, Returned, Transferred, Closing stock, Material reconciliation. Internal Reservation shall remain a distinct term.
- **FR-017** When an active Admin imports a section plan, the quantity allocation cap alone may be exceeded through a server-owned transaction witness exclusive to the import function. The plan shall retain its import batch identity. Direct writes and subsequent live edits retain the normal cap; all parentage, organisation, item and work-order state checks remain in effect.
- **NFR-001** The system shall use exact decimal strings (three quantity decimals) across RPCs and calculate means in SQL before rounding display to two percentage decimals.
- **NFR-002** The system shall page lists on the server (default 50, maximum 100), expose total count, and execute the work-order rollup as one set-based RPC without per-work-order requests. At 18,600 items and 12,000 material lines, warm local RPC p95 shall be below 500 ms over 20 reads; capture EXPLAIN ANALYZE and payload sizes. Item pickers shall search/page remotely.
- **NFR-003** Every new business table shall have organisation stamping, same-org composite references, RLS and restricted grants, reversible migrations and pgTAP proofs for each role and cross-org attempts. Imports shall audit source identity without exposing source content in public logs.
- **NFR-004** Screens shall follow `docs/ux/screen-rules.md` and DESIGN.md: figures then what waits then work; one left edge, tabular right-aligned quantities, 16px body/14px secondary, named pages in URL, accessible headers/focus/errors and phone horizontal table access. Loading, empty, error, denied, partial-import and over-supply states shall be designed and rendered.

## Non-goals

Calibration notices or serial equipment; purchasing, valuations or money posting; importing people columns; creating projects/locations; wholesale demo cleanup; guessing dates, work-order approval/value/state or counterpart transfers; storing workbook percentages as authority; an Excel formula evaluator; treating progress as installed quantity; replacing the general closing checklist.

## Owner questions (recommended answers)

1. Approve location sections and owner cost code at section level? **Yes**, as corrected by the coordinator; match and top up existing real sample data.
2. Accept historical over-supply while keeping live requisition limits? **Yes**, Admin import only, visibly flagged and audited.
3. Which date applies where a movement has no single date? **Require an explicit approved import-effective date per sheet in the private mapping**; label it as imported history, never pretend it is the source transaction date.
4. How should inconsistent source closing stock be handled? **Refuse the section and correct/map its evidence first**, rather than manufacture an adjustment to force parity.
5. Agree work-order reconciliation = lowest location step, with a location drilldown? **Yes**; preserve location percentages and avoid declaring a multi-location order reconciled early.
