# PMO Portal — product context

Register: product.
Platform: web; desktop-first, and usable at phone width.

## People
The first client is served by one owner-operator who currently operates both
PMO and ERPNext. Later users include project managers, finance teams and staff
at contract- and project-based organizations; the product is not industry-specific.

## Jobs
- Know which projects need attention and what to do next.
- See a project's trustworthy budget, spending, billing and payment position.
- Plan and deliver work, record time and expenses, and keep evidence together.
- Raise and send invoices, record payments and manage purchasing.
- Review a request with enough context to approve or return it confidently.
- Connect business tools once, then deal only with exceptions needing judgment.

## Product principles
- Calm by default: prioritize the current job; disclose secondary detail on demand.
- One main job per screen, with one clear next action and recognizable navigation.
- Plain language: describe business outcomes, never make people decode system internals.
- PMO is the user's screen over ERPNext, not a dashboard that sends them elsewhere to work.
- Integrations are set up once with proposed matches for review, then self-maintain.
  Ask again only for ambiguity, a consequential change or a genuinely new exception.
- Show money only when it is right. When information is missing, say what is missing
  in human terms and offer the next useful action; unknown is never zero.
- Keep financial consequences and safeguards visible without exposing implementation machinery.
- Preserve task context across screens and reduce repeated selection and confirmation.
- Earn trust through legible, consistent behavior rather than decorative AI styling.
